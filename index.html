About

The Designer

I’m Niko Mtema, an architectural designer whose path has been shaped by a deep interest in structures, building systems, and how they affect the people and environment around them.

Every decision I make starts from the psychology that drives and defines a space. I believe architecture should produce a greater good for humanity — and I aim to create spaces that define generations.
