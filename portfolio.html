My work is shaped by a deep interest in how people inhabit space — not only how a building looks, but how it feels to move through it.

I design around contrast and balance: grounded against light, geometric against organic, structure against fluidity. Opposing elements brought into a single, coherent whole.

I take a project through every stage — concept, 3D visualization, and a full set of working drawings — and I keep drawing by hand throughout, because the idea should be tested in pencil before any software opens.

Languages: Swahili, English  ·  Interests: Sketching, 3D art, animation, photography, football

Selected Work

Individual studio projects from the Bachelor of Architecture at Ardhi University, spanning commercial, residential, interior and freehand work.

Studio Project
Commercial · Hospitality·Ardhi University studio

A 5-Star Hotel

Defined by contrast and balance, the hotel is conceived around duality — opposing elements coexisting in harmony. A stone-clad podium anchors the building to its context while two refined towers rise above in glass and concrete, expressing lightness and precision. Clean geometric forms are softened by subtle organic curves, creating a dialogue between structure and fluidity.

Concept
Duality — grounded mass vs. rising lightness
Form
Stone podium, twin glass-and-concrete towers

Studio Project
Residential·350+ units

Affordable Housing

Over 350 residential units in a community-oriented plan. A colour-coded window-framing system distinguishes blocks and unit clusters through vibrant accents — improving wayfinding and unit identification without adding construction complexity.

Studio Project
Residential·Studio work

Residentials

A collection of residential studies exploring massing, daylight and the threshold between interior and garden — homes that open up as the day moves across them.

Studio Project
Interiors·Studio work

Interior Design

Interiors where colour and texture carry the mood — sage green and burnt orange against warm plaster, daylight and lamplight studied as carefully as the furniture. Spaces built digitally first, then resolved down to material and finish.

Studio Project
Interiors·Double-height living

Marble Interior

Double-height living anchored by marble — a stair clad in book-matched stone, a media wall framed in marble, a freestanding bath set against the skyline, a private cinema under a starlit ceiling. Contrast between stone, light and void sets the entire register of the space.

Freehand·Pencil & ink

Hand Sketches

A complete hand-drafted set for the cafeteria — site plan at 1:200, floor plans, measured sections, construction details down to 1:2, coloured elevations and a colour-pencil perspective. Where every project begins: pencil, ink and a steady hand, before any software opens.

From first sketch
to working drawings

Commercial, residential or interior — full service across every stage of the design.

01

Concept & planning

By hand first — site plans, sections and studies test the idea before any software opens, so the bones are right before the polish begins.

02

3D visualization

You see and walk the finished space before a wall goes up — built digitally in Lumion, D5 and V-Ray so there are no surprises on site.

03

Working drawings

A full, buildable package modelled in Revit and ArchiCAD that a contractor can price and construct from — every stage covered.

04

Interior concept

Colour, texture and material chosen for the mood of the room — a space that feels considered, not decorated.

05

Residential design

From a single home to 350+ unit housing schemes — density planned so it never costs the resident their light or privacy.

06

Visual & graphic work

Boards, diagrams and presentation graphics in the Adobe suite — the idea communicated as clearly as it's designed.
